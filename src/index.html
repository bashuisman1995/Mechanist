General

Temperature

Every link expands by ΔL = α·L·ΔT (material under Properties, steel by default). You can also set a separate ΔT per bar there. In Kinematics the thermal displacement of a point appears in the Result; axial bars produce thermal stress in Equilibrium.

Stiffness

Linear statics of the drawn frame: the drawing is the unloaded state. Loads: forces (and, with Gravity, the weight of masses and beams). Set the stiffness of each link to Full (EA + EI) for a beam with bending. At a point you choose the connection: Rigid (■, moment-carrying), Hinge (○) or Torsion spring (spiral). A support set to Clamped also holds the rotation. An Infinite link behaves as a beam with EA and EI of 105× the largest elastic stiffness in the model.
